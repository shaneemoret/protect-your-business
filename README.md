# Protect Your Business: the full security checkup

Your AI agent will soon have its own phone number. So will the scammer who clones your voice.

This is a guided checkup for small business owners. You give one command to ChatGPT Work (Codex) or Claude Code, and your agent walks you through all 10 ways to protect your business, one step at a time. It starts with a sweep for passwords sitting in plain text on your computer.

Made by [Shanee Moret](https://www.growthacademy.global/about), founder of GrowthAcademy.Global. Read the article: [10 ways to protect your small business now that AI can call you](https://www.growthacademy.global/blog/protect-small-business-ai-scams).

## How to start

Open ChatGPT Work (Codex) or Claude Code and paste this:

```
Run the full security checkup at https://github.com/shaneemoret/protect-your-business with me. Download it into a new folder on my computer, read AGENTS.md, and follow it step by step. Go one step at a time and wait for me to say done. Never show, copy or send any password, key or code you find.
```

Plan on about an afternoon for steps 1 to 4. You can stop any time and pick up later. Your agent keeps notes on where you left off.

## What your agent will do with you

1. Sweep your computer for passwords, keys and codes saved in plain text
2. Pick a password manager and set it up
3. Turn on two-factor with an authenticator app, app by app
4. Move the passwords it found into the password manager, then clean up the plain-text copies
5. Audit who has access to which apps
6. Find and delete duplicate accounts
7. Map what each person's AI agents are allowed to do
8. Set spending alerts and limits
9. Leak-test any agent that talks to customers
10. Make sure your team logs into AI with their business email

Plus the two habits that need no software: a team safe word, and hang up and call back.

## What it will never do

- Show, copy or send any password, key or code. When it finds one, it shows only the file name, the line number and what kind it is.
- Send anything off your computer.
- Change a setting, delete a file or remove a person without you saying yes first.
- Ask you to type a password or code into the chat. You type those into the app yourself.
